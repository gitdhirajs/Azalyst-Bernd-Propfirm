# Session 2026-09-27 — why the FundingPips paper trades failed, and the live fix

## 1. What was found

**Source of the trades.** Every post in Discord `#azalyst-fundingpip-challenge` (244 of 244)
came from GitHub Actions runs of **`gitdhirajs/Azalyst-Bernd-Propfirm` main** (the original repo).
`gitdhirajsv` (contributor account) had its scan workflows disabled since 2026-07-07.

**The account never breached.** Three $5k challenges (reset 13 Jul, 28 Jul, 14 Sep); worst loss used
$70 of $300. Ledger across them: 24 closed trades = 2 wins, 9 losses, 13 closed at $0.
No real winner: both "wins" were not genuine (CL=F never traded at its entry; DAX exit mis-priced).

**Root causes (verified by 8 independent agents against code, state history, run logs, market data):**

| # | Defect | Effect on the account |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Yahoo **daily forex Close is wrong** (≈ copy of the Open). Median body 0–2%, zero decisive candles in a year | Bot could not see real FX zones on the daily chart; zones were built on the unfinished candle |
| 2 | Zones/patterns detected on the **still-forming candle** | Broke rule #4 "never anticipate" |
| 3 | Distance gate used `abs(close − entry)` | Orders sent when price was already past the entry, even past the stop; booked as filled at a price the market wasn't at. 6 of 8 losing setups |
| 4 | Pattern entries (E3b, buy-stop above a hammer high) treated as **limit** orders | "Filled" exactly when the setup had failed |
| 5 | Positions priced against **one daily candle per run** | Fills from lows printed before the order; breakeven armed and stopped on the same candle → 5 real winners closed at $0; once-a-day schedule saw ~6h of each FX day (USDJPY stop hit 24 Sep never registered) |
| 6 | No weekend/market-hours guard | Trades opened and closed on Sat/Sun on Friday's prices (both EURNZD losses) |
| 7 | NaN price passed the distance gate | Created the fake CL=F order |
| 8 | zone_id changed daily | Closed setups re-traded (EURNZD lost twice) |
| 9 | Fixed 0.01 lots | $1–52 risk per trade; the 6% target needed ~40R |
| 10 | Half-T1 breakeven rule | 5 of 13 $0 closes were genuine retraces to entry (rule choice, not a bug) |

Also: the 28 Jul manual reset deleted 16 open positions unrecorded (6 later reached T3).

**Chart check (TradingView hourly, 23 distinct trades):** orders opened at once into the zone
netted −3.3R; resting orders netted +10R. Direction after 10 days was right 13 of 20, but only
10 of 20 reached +1R before the stop — entries were early. Interactive page:
https://claude.ai/artifact/CnbnV5GYxP8YqaA1tAF1rz

## 2. User decisions

- Original repo = **gitdhirajs**; the live bot must run the same engine/method as this folder.
- **Risk 1% per trade** ($50), fixed_lot_mode off, capped at 1.5% after lot rounding.
- **Fills/stops/targets from replayed 1h candles** (zones and entries still weekly + daily).
- **Trade management: stop never moves, 100% closes at T2 (2R).** No breakeven, partial or trailing.
- Fresh $5k challenge on deploy. Merge to main after tests pass.
