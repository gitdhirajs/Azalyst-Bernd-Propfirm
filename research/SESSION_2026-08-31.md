## 2. The defect, located -- but the significance was withdrawn (C-119, C-127)

**WITHDRAWN.** Reading the source frames with vision (rather than trusting the extracted

    fully collapsed (34 setups)     4/0   p=0.1250  ns

The DIRECTION never reverses across any rule. The significance does not survive.

The engine already does that -- it rests a limit at the zone proximal. It gets the
opposite sign:

    population                       zone-entry wins   market wins   p
    our engine, reachability arm           2               5        0.453
    our engine, reachable + midpoint       4               5        1.000
    THEIR signals corpus                   8               1        0.039

Fisher exact on those splits gave p=0.035 -- but it used the un-deduplicated 8:1. Against
the collapsed 4:0 the comparison weakens accordingly. **The qualitative claim survives**
(same mechanic, opposite sign, never reversing); "significantly different" does not.

**The edge is in WHICH ZONE is chosen, not in the entry technique.** Our zone selection is
not merely worse than theirs -- it is inverted.

## 5. Tooling that now guards all of it

`goldtest/verdict.py` -- four gates, weakest result decides. Every false positive in this
project passed at least one honest-looking test, so they are run together:

    1 accuracy, paired McNemar on cases scored in BOTH arms
    2 did the emitted TRADE POPULATION change (invalidates outcome comparison)
    3 expectancy vs a DRIFT-MATCHED null, never the driftless 1/3
    4 does the zone entry beat the same call at market, paired

All five arms tested to date return NOT supported. Exit code gates a commit.

Two bugs found while building it, both the class it exists to prevent: cases with
`verdict=None` counted as misses (attrition bias), and a normal approximation reported
**p=0.032 on a 2-of-2 result** where the exact binomial is 0.135. All proportion tests
now use the exact distribution with an n>=10 requirement.
