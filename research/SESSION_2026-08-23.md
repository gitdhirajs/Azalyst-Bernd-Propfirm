### The deadlock

`risk.fixed_lot_mode: true` (set 2026-07-13 after the 1-Step breach) pins every signal to
0.01 lots. Measured actual risk over 66 signals: **min 0.02%, median 0.14%, mean 0.21%,
max 0.70%** — against an intended 1%. 67 of 69 lot lines are `0.01`.

Its own unlock condition, from the config comment:

> bump `fixed_lot_multiplier` only AFTER you've closed at least one trade in profit

E-03 prevents a profitable close; E-02 prevents one being recorded. **The unlock can never
fire.** The August account sat at exactly $5,000.00 for 22 days — locked, not idle.

### Arithmetic

```
Bernd  : 74% direction (this repo's own figure) x 1% risk x 2R  ->  +1.22%/trade -> 6% in ~5 trades
Live   : 0.14% risk, 0 trades ever reached 1R                   ->  ~+0.07%/trade -> 6% in ~90 trades
```

Even with the directional edge fully intact, the account is sized 7–8x too small to pass a
6% challenge.

### New: `gemini/vision_pass.py` — semantic reading as a SECOND pass

Same key pool, same model chain, full resolution. Three structural constraints:

1. **Separate output** — `*.vision.jsonl`, never the transcription `.jsonl`.
2. **No new numbers** — every number is a verbatim on-screen quote tagged `source_kind`
   (`legend` / `settings_dialog` / `date_axis` / `price_axis` / `drawn_label` / `other`).
3. **Every claim carries evidence** — a field with no `evidence` entry is **dropped** by
   `sanity_check_vision()`, not trusted. Non-chart frames have setup fields force-cleared.

Returns what the transcriber cannot: `zone_drawn`, `price_vs_zone`, `implied_direction`,
`structure`, `drawn_objects`, `indicator_panes`, `what_is_being_demonstrated`.

**Measured — n=8 frames, Location chapter. This is a smoke test, not a benchmark.**

`STRICT_PROMPT` has no right-axis price field, so those numbers were never recorded to compare

**n=2. That proves the mechanism runs end-to-end and nothing else.** A real agreement rate
needs a full chapter run + `--verify`.
